Privacy Policyగోప్యతా విధానం

How Mana Guruji collects, uses and protects your personal information.మీ వ్యక్తిగత సమాచారాన్ని మేము ఎలా సేకరిస్తాం, ఉపయోగిస్తాం, రక్షిస్తాం.

ఈ విధానం ఆంగ్లంలో ఉంది. ఏదైనా సందేహం ఉంటే ఆంగ్ల పాఠమే చెల్లుబాటు అవుతుంది. తెలుగులో వివరణ కోసం మమ్మల్ని సంప్రదించండి.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Information we collect

  • Booking details: name, mobile number, email, address, preferred date and time, and ritual details you choose to share (such as gothram or nakshatram).
  • Payment information: processed by Razorpay. We receive the payment status and transaction reference, not your full card or bank details.
  • Device information: basic technical data such as browser type and app version, used to keep the Platform working.
  • Pandit information: for pandits, identity and KYC documents needed for verification and payouts.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm and manage your booking and assign a pandit.
  • To share your name, address and booking details with the assigned pandit so they can perform the service.
  • To process payments, refunds and pandit payouts.
  • To send booking updates by SMS, WhatsApp, email or app notification.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Sharing

We share information only with the assigned pandit, our payment processor (Razorpay), SMS and messaging providers, and cloud hosting providers who process data on our behalf, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on secure servers with access limited to authorised staff. KYC documents are kept in private storage. We keep booking records for as long as required for accounting and legal purposes.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, subject to records we must keep by law. Write to us at the email below.

6. Cookies

The website uses only essential browser storage, for example to remember your language choice. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. Contact and Grievance Officer

For privacy questions or grievances, write to the Grievance Officer, Mana Guruji, Hyderabad, Telangana 500083, India, at support@managuruji.in. We acknowledge every grievance within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days.
