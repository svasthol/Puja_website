ఈ విధానం ఆంగ్లంలో ఉంది. ఏదైనా సందేహం ఉంటే ఆంగ్ల పాఠమే చెల్లుబాటు అవుతుంది. తెలుగులో వివరణ కోసం మమ్మల్ని సంప్రదించండి.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Cancelling a booking

You can cancel a booking from the Mana Guruji app, or by calling +91 [10-digit phone] or emailing support@managuruji.in with your booking details. Refunds depend on when you cancel:

When you cancelRefund
Before a pandit is assigned100% of the amount paid
After a pandit is assigned, more than 24 hours before the puja90% of the amount paid
After a pandit is assigned, within 24 hours of the puja50% of the amount paid
After the puja has started, or after it is completedNot eligible for cancellation

Times are calculated in Indian Standard Time against the scheduled start time of the puja.

2. Cancellations by us

  • If we cannot assign a pandit for your booking, we cancel it and refund 100% of the amount paid.
  • If the assigned pandit cancels, we first try to assign another verified pandit for the same time. If we cannot, you receive a 100% refund.
  • If the pandit does not arrive and we are unable to arrange a replacement, you receive a 100% refund.

3. Rescheduling

You may reschedule once at no charge if you inform us more than 24 hours before the puja, subject to pandit availability.

4. Samagri kit

If you cancel before the puja day, the samagri kit amount is refunded in full. Once the kit has been brought to the venue, the kit amount is not refundable.

5. Service complaints

If the service was not performed as described, contact us within 48 hours of the scheduled time. We review each case and may offer a partial or full refund.

6. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are initiated within 2 working days and credited to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the credit. Amounts paid by you directly to a pandit outside the Platform are not refundable through us.
